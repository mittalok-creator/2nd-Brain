## Cover image direction

Notion page covers are the largest visual element in the workspace, seen many times a day.
They must be quiet:

- Dark, low-contrast, near-monochrome.
- No text — the page title already says what the page is.
- Abstract over illustrative. Suggestive of structure rather than depicting a brain.
- Consistent treatment across every page, varying only in hue by life area.

A cover that draws attention is a cover that becomes irritating within a week.
