Chapter 1

Aap 70% Notion pehle se jaante ho

Excel ke saath jitna time bitaya hai, utna hi Notion samajhna aasan hoga. Bas naam badalte hain, concept nahi. Chaar buniyaadi mappings:

Excel meinNotion meinFarak

Sheet (neeche tab)Page Pages ke andar aur pages ja sakte hain, sheets mein nahi
Table (Ctrl+T)Database Aapki Diary Log, Tasks, Goals — sab databases hain
Column headerProperty Har column ka ek fixed type hota hai (neeche Chapter 3)
Aapke paas already ek example hai. Aapki 📓 Diary Log 2026 database Excel ke us table jaisi hai jismein columns hain: Task, Date, Time, Done, Remark. Aapne khud BDWO REPORT aur BDWO CLAIM naam ki do rows daali — bilkul waise jaise Excel mein do naye rows type karte ho.

Sabse bada farak — jo abhi samajhna hai

Excel mein row sirf data hai. Notion mein har row khud ek page hai — poori document, khulne wali. Agla chapter yahi dikhata hai, aapki apni BDWO REPORT entry se.

Chapter 2

Chapter 3

Column ka type fix hota hai

Excel ki sabse aam problem: ek column mein kabhi "Yes", kabhi "Y", kabhi "TRUE" — sab chal jaata hai, koi rok nahi. Notion mein har column ka ek fixed type hota hai, aur wo type hamesha wahi values maanta hai.

Aapki Diary Log ke 5 columns, aapki asli do rows ke saath

TaskDateTimeDoneRemark
BDWO REPORT27 Jul 20264:00 PM☐FINACLE
BDWO CLAIM30 Jul 20265:00 PM☐FROM BRANCHES TO HO
ColumnTypeIska matlab
TaskTitle Har row ka naam. Ye hamesha text hota hai — jaise Excel ka column A.
DateDate Hamesha ek asli calendar date. "27/7" ya "next monday" nahi likh sakte — Notion khud date-picker dikhata hai, ghalat format ho hi nahi sakta.
TimeText Jaan-bujh kar plain text rakha hai, taaki "4 baje" ya "4:00 PM" jaisa likhna aasan rahe — par isiliye consistency haath se rakhni padti hai.
DoneCheckbox Sirf do value: tick ya khaali. "haan"/"done"/"ho gaya" possible hi nahi.
RemarkText Free text — jo bhi likhna hai.
Maine abhi ek cheez fix ki — aur ye is chapter ka sabse achha example hai.
Aapne BDWO REPORT mein Time "4:00 pm" likha, aur BDWO CLAIM mein "5:00 pM". Dono chalte the (koi error nahi aaya) kyunki Time ek Text field hai — jo bhi likho, wo accept karta hai.

Yahi Excel wali purani problem hai: field free-text ho toh consistency sirf discipline se aati hai, system se nahi. Maine dono ko 4:00 PM aur 5:00 PM kar diya — ab ek jaisa dikhta hai. Agar Date field ki tarah Time bhi strict hota, ye galti ho hi nahi sakti thi.

Chapter 4

Relation, VLOOKUP nahi

Aapki Diary mein relations nahi hain — jaan-bujh kar, Diary simple rakhi gayi hai. Par aapke 2nd Brain ke Tasks database mein hain, aur wahi is concept ke liye sabse achha example hai: Deepak wala case.

VLOOKUP dhoondh ke laata hai (ek-tarfa). Relation jodta hai (do-tarfa, hamesha zinda)
Rollup = jaise SUMIF doosri sheet se, par automatic. Aapki Goals mein "Progress" isi tarah Projects se apne aap aata hai — kabhi haath se nahi bharna.
Chapter 5

View = Filter jo yaad rehta hai

Excel mein: Filter lagate ho, dekhte ho, hata dete ho. Notion mein: filter ek baar banao, naam do, hamesha ke liye tab ban jaata hai.

Aapki apni Diary ke Overdue/Today/Future dashboard mein aapki dono entries abhi kahan khadi hain:

Aapki BDWO REPORT aur BDWO CLAIM — dashboard ne khud sahi column mein rakh diya
Kuch bharna nahi pada. Aapne bas Date daali; system ne khud tay kiya ki kaunsi entry "Today" hai aur kaunsi "Future". Yehi ek achhi view ka fayda hai — Excel mein har baar dobara filter lagana padta, yahan ek baar set hone ke baad hamesha sahi rehta hai.
Chapter 6

Aapki dono entries ka review

Aapne poocha tha "check karo sahi hai kya" — yahan poora review, ek jagah:

Overall: sab kuch sahi hai. Sirf Time formatting mein farak tha, wo fix kar diya gaya. Data-wise koi galti nahi thi.

Ek chhota sujhaav, aage ke liye

Jab bhi Time likho, hamesha ek hi format use karo — jaise 4:00 PM. Ye system rokta nahi (Text field hai), par khud ka niyam bana lena isi tarah ki cheezein hamesha saaf rakhta hai.
