# User Guide

How to actually operate 2nd Brain day to day.

> **Status** — ⬜ Written in Phase 8, once the workspace, dashboards, and automations it
> describes exist. The operating model below is the settled intent; the detailed procedures
> follow.

---

## The operating model

The system runs on four loops of increasing scope. Each loop feeds the one above it.

| Loop | Cadence | Duration | Question it answers |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Capture** | Continuous | Seconds | What just came in? |
| **Daily** | Morning and evening | 5 + 5 min | What matters today? What happened? |
| **Weekly** | Once a week | 30 min | Am I moving on what I said mattered? |
| **Monthly** | Once a month | 60 min | Is the direction still right? |

Nothing else is required. If the system needs more attention than this to stay useful, the
system is wrong, not the user.

---

## Capture

One inbox. Everything enters through it — a thought, a link, a task, a name, a receipt.
Capture is deliberately dumb: no categorising at capture time, because friction at capture
is what kills personal systems.

Routing to the owning entity happens during the daily loop, assisted by an agent.

---

## Daily

**Morning (5 minutes)** — the Today dashboard shows the day's commitments, the tasks that
advance a live goal, and today's habits. An agent has already drafted a plan overnight; the
morning is for accepting or adjusting it, not for building it from scratch.

**Evening (5 minutes)** — log what happened, close what closed, note what slipped and why.
The slip reason is the valuable part: it is the raw material for the weekly review.

---

## Weekly

Thirty minutes, one dashboard. Progress against goals, habit consistency, what got dropped,
and the honest question of whether the week's work was aimed at the right thing. Produces a
short written review and, usually, one or two adjustments.

---

## Monthly

An hour. Trends rather than events. Goal viability, area balance, financial and health
direction, learning throughput, and a decision review — checking past decisions against
their actual outcomes. Produces a written review and, where warranted, changes to goals.
